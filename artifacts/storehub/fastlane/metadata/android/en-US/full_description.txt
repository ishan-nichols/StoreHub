StoreHub POS is a complete point-of-sale system built for modern retail businesses.

<b>SELL FASTER</b>
• Barcode scanning for instant product lookup
• Lightning-fast checkout with cash, card, and digital payments
• Split payments and custom discounts

<b>MANAGE YOUR STORE</b>
• Real-time inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
• Product catalogue with variants, categories, and pricing
• Purchase orders and supplier management

<b>UNDERSTAND YOUR BUSINESS</b>
• Live sales dashboard and daily revenue summaries
• Best-seller reports, profit margins, and trend analysis
• Tax reporting and end-of-day reconciliation

<b>BUILT FOR TEAMS</b>
• Role-based access: owners, managers, and cashiers
• Employee shift tracking and performance reports
• Multi-store support from a single account

<b>HARDWARE READY</b>
• Bluetooth card reader support
• Receipt printer via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
• Kiosk mode locks the app to the screen on dedicated POS hardware

StoreHub POS works fully offline — sales keep processing even without internet, and everything syncs automatically when you reconnect.
