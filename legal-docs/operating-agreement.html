This Operating Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 28, 2026, by and among the Member(s) identified herein, for the purpose of organizing and governing StoreHub LLC, a Limited Liability Company ("Company"), formed pursuant to the laws of the State of Delaware (or the applicable state of formation).

Article 1 — Organization
1.1 Formation

The Company has been organized as a Limited Liability Company under the laws of the State of Delaware (or applicable state) by filing Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State. The rights and obligations of the Members and the administration of the Company shall be governed by this Agreement and the applicable state LLC Act.

1.2 Company Name

The business of the Company shall be conducted under the name StoreHub LLC, and under such assumed or trade names as may be determined by the Managing Member from time to time.

1.3 Principal Place of Business

The principal office of the Company shall be located at such place as the Managing Member shall determine. The Company may also maintain such other offices as the Managing Member may deem necessary.

1.4 Term

The Company shall have a perpetual existence unless dissolved pursuant to the terms of this Agreement or applicable law.

1.5 Purpose

The Company is organized for any lawful business purpose permissible under the applicable state LLC Act, including but not limited to software development, retail technology solutions, point-of-sale systems, business management tools, and all related activities.

Article 2 — Members and Capital Contributions
2.1 Initial Member

The Company shall initially have one (1) Member. The name, address, and ownership interest of the initial Member are as follows:

Member Name Membership Interest Capital Contribution Role
Ishan Nichols 100% As recorded in Company books Managing Member
2.2 Capital Contributions

The initial capital contribution of the Member shall be recorded in the Company's books and records. No Member shall be required to make any additional capital contribution without their express written consent. The Company may accept additional capital contributions from the Member at any time.

2.3 No Interest on Capital

No Member shall be entitled to receive interest on their capital contribution unless expressly agreed to in writing by all Members.

2.4 Return of Capital

No Member shall have the right to demand or receive the return of their capital contribution except upon dissolution of the Company or as otherwise agreed in writing.

2.5 Single-Member Status

As a single-member LLC, the Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity for U.S. federal income tax purposes unless the Member elects otherwise. The Member shall be solely responsible for reporting the Company's income, deductions, and credits on their individual tax return (Schedule C or applicable form).

Article 3 — Management
3.1 Member-Managed

The Company shall be Member-Managed. The Managing Member shall have full authority to manage, control, administer, and operate the business and affairs of the Company.

3.2 Managing Member

Ishan Nichols is hereby designated as the Managing Member with full authority to act on behalf of the Company, including but not limited to:

  • Entering into contracts, agreements, and commitments on behalf of the Company;
  • Opening and managing bank accounts and financial instruments;
  • Hiring, supervising, disciplining, and terminating employees and independent contractors;
  • Acquiring, leasing, or disposing of Company assets;
  • Borrowing money and pledging Company assets as collateral;
  • Executing legal documents, filings, and instruments;
  • Making all tax elections and filings on behalf of the Company;
  • Any other act necessary or appropriate to carry out the Company's business.
3.3 Limitations on Authority

The Managing Member shall not, without documented authorization:

  • Admit new Members to the Company;
  • Merge or consolidate the Company with another entity;
  • Dissolve or wind up the Company (except as permitted by law);
  • Amend this Operating Agreement.
Article 4 — Employees
4.1 Employment Authority

The Managing Member has sole authority to hire, retain, compensate, and terminate employees, contractors, and agents of the Company.

4.2 Employees Are Not Members

Employment by the Company does not confer Membership interest or ownership rights. Employees have no ownership stake, voting rights, or right to distributions unless separately agreed in writing and documented as a Membership interest transfer.

4.4 Compensation

Employee compensation, benefits, and terms of employment shall be set by the Managing Member and documented separately in an employment agreement, offer letter, or other appropriate instrument.

Article 5 — Allocations and Distributions
5.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses

All profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated 100% to Ishan Nichols as the sole Member.

5.2 Distributions

The Managing Member shall determine the timing and amount of distributions in their sole discretion. Distributions shall be made to the Member in proportion to their ownership interest.

5.3 Tax Distributions

To the extent practicable, the Company shall make distributions to the Member in amounts sufficient to cover the Member's estimated income tax liability arising from the Company's operations.

5.4 No Distributions in Violation of Law

No distribution shall be made if, after giving effect to the distribution, the Company would not be able to pay its debts as they become due in the ordinary course of business.

Article 6 — Books, Records, and Accounting
6.1 Books and Records

The Company shall maintain accurate and complete books and records of account, including all financial statements, bank records, tax filings, contracts, and other documents. Records shall be maintained at the principal place of business.

6.2 Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the Company shall be the calendar year (January 1 through December 31), unless otherwise determined by the Managing Member for tax purposes.

6.3 Accounting Method

The Company shall use the accounting method as determined by the Managing Member, in consultation with the Company's accountant.

Article 7 — Transfers of Membership Interest
7.1 Transfer by Managing Member

Ishan Nichols, as the sole Member, may transfer all or any portion of their Membership interest at their sole discretion. Any such transfer shall be documented in writing and, to be effective, shall comply with all applicable state LLC statutes.

7.2 Admission of New Members

New Members may be admitted upon the written consent of the existing Member(s) and upon such terms and conditions as the Member(s) shall determine.

Article 8 — Dissolution and Winding Up
8.1 Events of Dissolution

The Company shall be dissolved upon:

  • The written election of the Managing Member to dissolve;
  • The occurrence of any event that causes dissolution under applicable state law;
  • A judicial decree of dissolution.
8.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution, the Managing Member shall wind up the Company's affairs, pay or make provision for all Company liabilities, and distribute remaining assets to the Member(s) in proportion to their ownership interests.

Article 9 — Indemnification and Liability
9.1 Indemnification

The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Managing Member and any authorized agent acting on behalf of the Company from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, costs, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from acts taken in good faith on behalf of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

9.2 Limitation of Liability

No Member shall be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a Member, except as otherwise required by law.

Article 10 — Miscellaneous
10.1 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether oral or written.

10.2 Amendments

This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Managing Member.

10.3 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware (or the applicable state of formation), without regard to conflict of law principles.

10.4 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

10.5 Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be valid.
